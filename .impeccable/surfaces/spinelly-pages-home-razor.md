# Home (spinelly.net)

Scope: the single-page portfolio, Experience mode. Recruiters and prospective clients, cold, often on mobile. Job: see live shipped work, open it, or reach out. Constraints: Blazor WASM + MudBlazor, owner's copy verbatim, cassette/TV showcase kept, typing intro retired by the owner, no gradients, glows or offset shadows.

Memorable moment: lower-third straps wiping in, and a station bug that always says what is on.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a broadcast channel. Sections are programmes, projects are tapes aired on the house TV. Refuses the dark-hero-plus-card-grid dev portfolio.

OWN-WORLD: Studio black #0f0e16 ground, phosphor white type. Solid violet #b3a6ff and teal #9ff2dc lower-third straps carry headings, cut tight to the words like broadcast name straps; the teal tier carries a real fact or is omitted. Tally red only means LIVE. Archivo stretched wide for idents, condensed for straps; Martian Mono for clock and timecodes. Square corners, no blur glows.
